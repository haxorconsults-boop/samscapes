Our Story

Rooted in Passion

Discover the vision and expertise behind Nairobi's most exquisite outdoor spaces.

Cultivating Beauty
Since 2017

Samscape's Solutions began with a simple belief: that every outdoor space has the potential to elevate the human spirit.

Founded in Karen, Nairobi, we've spent the last seven years transforming ordinary yards into extraordinary sanctuaries. We are a collective of horticulturists, landscape architects, and craftsmen who share a profound respect for nature and a commitment to meticulous design.

Our approach is deeply personal. We don't just plant gardens; we curate living environments that reflect your lifestyle and harmonize with the local ecology. From the initial soil assessment to the final placement of a feature stone, our attention to detail is unwavering.
