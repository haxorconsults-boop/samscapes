Who We Are

A Top Landscaping Company in Kenya

Samscapes Solution is a company registered as a limited company. The name simply means Bringing Solutions to landscapes. We are passionate about the environment and we love to see everything green.

Our team is made up of visionary men and women who love nature. With over ten years' experience across the country, you can depend on us to deliver high-quality, dependable landscaping and gardening services.

Whether you need a serene water feature, comprehensive hardscaping, or regular garden care, we approach every project with meticulous love for detail.
