Our Services

Expert solutions for beautiful, breathtaking gardens.

Garden Design

Samscapes, we design and implement your ideas, transforming them into beautiful water landscapes. In gardening, we have a wide variety of products to realize any vision.

  • Flowers & Trees
  • Ground covers & Herbs
  • Fruits, Soil, Manure, Grass, Compost
  • Natural fence & Indigenous trees
  • Flower pots (pots, troughs)
  • Baskets (hanging & rectangular)

Landscaping

We transform your dreams into ideas, and then finally beautiful breath-taking gardens. Our comprehensive landscaping services ensure that every element of your outdoor space is perfectly balanced and aesthetically pleasing.

Garden Care and Maintenance

After we transform your ideas into lovely gardens, we are there to ensure your garden grows and flourishes. Regular maintenance, pruning, fertilizing, and seasonal care are handled by our expert team to keep your landscape looking pristine year-round.

Water Features

Every great idea can be transformed into a flowing water fall! We offer creative water features including waterfalls, fountains, small ponds, fish ponds, streams, and indoor container water gardens that bring tranquility and life to your space.

Hardcape

We assess your needs and give you solutions best suited for you. No job is too big or too small. From patios and walkways to retaining walls and structural elements, our hardscaping provides the solid foundation your landscape needs.

Irrigation

Nothing brings out more beauty like a beautiful landscape or evergreen garden during the dry season. We design and install efficient irrigation systems tailored to the specific needs of your plants and lawn to conserve water and ensure optimal growth.
